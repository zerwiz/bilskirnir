# 2026-11-22 — talk to the agent while watching her computer

The Allfather: *"in Nanna's computer I want to be able to start, pause and stop the streaming voice, so I can tell Nanna what to do and see her mouse move around on the screen — and all her work being done in Nanna's computer."*

The computer viewer's top bar now carries the voice: **● Talk** starts the live call with that agent; while it runs, **❚❚ Pause / ▶ Resume** and **■ Stop**, with her state (listening · thinking · speaks · paused). *Pause* closes the mic and silences her voice (her queued speech is dropped), while the call and her work go on; *Resume* opens both again. Her pointer is always drawn on the watched screen (`showDotCursor`), so its movement is visible. The call already hands real work to her turn queue, and a turn dresses and uses her own screen first (`screenForWork`), so the work happens on the computer being watched.
